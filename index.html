Infinite Alchemy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Infinite Alchemy ("the game") is an offline game about mixing elements. It is made for everyone, including children, and is published by Muhammad Kamal (oyekamal) ("we", "us"). This page explains, in plain words, what the game does with information.

1. The short version

  • No accounts. You do not sign in, and we do not ask for a name, email, phone number, age, photo, contacts, microphone, camera or location.
  • No tracking by us. The game has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no servers of ours. The game sends nothing to us.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. The ads are set to child-directed treatment: they are not personalised, they are limited to content rated for everyone (rating "G"), and the game does not ask for the Android advertising ID.
  • Links that leave the game are behind a "grown-ups only" question.

2. What is stored on the device

The game saves your progress (elements found, chapter, coins, settings, and a few counters such as when an ad was last shown, so ads cannot appear too often) in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. If Android backup is switched on for the device, Google may include the app's saved progress in the device backup under Google's own terms.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, full-screen and optional "watch a video" ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google LLC). To show an ad over the internet, the SDK has to contact Google, and Google receives technical information that every internet request carries, such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system and app version. Because the game is child-directed:

  • ads are not personalised: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing;
  • only ads suitable for all ages (rating "G") are requested;
  • the game does not request the Android advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app);
  • there is no behavioural targeting of any kind from our side.

Google handles this data under Google's Privacy Policy and its rules for child-directed ads. We do not receive any of it and we do not combine it with anything else.

"Watch a video" rewarded ads are always optional and clearly labelled. Nobody has to watch one to play.

4. Children

Children are welcome to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, because the game has no way to collect it. There is no chat, no user-created content, no social feature, no account and no purchase in the game.

  • Ads: shown with Google's child-directed settings and the lowest content rating, as described above.
  • Leaving the game: any link to a website or to the store page (for example this policy, or "Rate us") first asks a short question that an adult can answer: type the number shown in words. This keeps children from leaving the game by accident.
  • No in-app purchases: there is nothing to buy.

Parents: if you have any worry about this game, write to the address in section 9.

5. How to delete the data

All game data is on the device. Clear the app's storage (Android Settings, Apps, Infinite Alchemy, Storage, Clear data) or uninstall the game, and the data is gone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing on our side to delete. The in-game Settings also has a way to reset progress.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. The only outside company involved is Google, through the ad SDK in section 3.

7. Security

The game needs no sign-in and keeps no data on our systems. The ad SDK uses encrypted connections.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will change the date above and, for material changes, say so in the release notes.
